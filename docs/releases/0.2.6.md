Capacity Notch 0.2.6, a beta for Apple Silicon on macOS 14 or later.

**Updating from 0.2.5:** quit the running copy, replace `CapacityNotch.app`
in Applications with the new one, and open it. macOS will likely ask for
**Open Anyway** in System Settings → Privacy & Security again, since this
build is not notarized. The permissions you granted are kept.

What's new:

- **Volume on the music page.** At the right of the controls: the bar sets
  the Mac's volume and the speaker mutes and unmutes it. It follows the
  keyboard and Control Center, and it asks for no permission. An output
  whose volume macOS cannot set, such as some displays, shows no bar.
- **Geist on the surface.** The surface, the music row and the Teleprompter
  are set in Geist, as Settings already were. The figures in the open strip
  are the same size as in the closed one.

What's fixed:

- A click on the music bar moves the track there and the bar stays put,
  instead of springing back and jumping forward.
