Capacity Notch 0.2.5, a beta for Apple Silicon on macOS 14 or later.

**Updating from 0.2.4:** quit the running copy, replace `CapacityNotch.app`
in Applications with the new one, and open it. macOS will likely ask for
**Open Anyway** in System Settings → Privacy & Security again, since this
build is not notarized. The permissions you granted are kept.

What's new:

- **Softer shoulders.** Where the surface meets the menu bar the curve is
  twice as generous and drawn as part of the surface itself.
- **It rises to meet the pointer.** As the pointer comes near the closed
  surface it grows a little, and settles back when the pointer moves away.
  Opening still waits for the pointer to rest on it.

What's fixed:

- On a 14-inch MacBook Pro at its default scaling, the curve at each side of
  the closed surface no longer overlaps its corner by a hair.
