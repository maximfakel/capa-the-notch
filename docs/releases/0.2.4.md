Capacity Notch 0.2.4, a beta for Apple Silicon on macOS 14 or later.

**Updating from 0.2.3:** quit the running copy, replace `CapacityNotch.app`
in Applications with the new one, and open it. macOS will likely ask for
**Open Anyway** in System Settings → Privacy & Security again, since this
build is not notarized. The permissions you granted are kept.

What's new — the closed strip fits the notch it sits on:

- **Narrower, and sized to the Mac.** 370 points wide on a MacBook Pro at
  its default scaling, 410 at More Space, instead of 422 everywhere. On a
  14-inch at the default scaling it takes a quarter of the menu bar rather
  than more.
- **Shoulders.** Where the surface meets the menu bar it curves into it,
  closed and open, instead of meeting it at a corner.
- A 22-point corner and twelve points to each edge, as drawn.
