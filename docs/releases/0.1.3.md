Capacity Notch 0.1.3, a beta for Apple Silicon on macOS 14 or later.

**Updating from 0.1.2:** quit the running copy, replace `CapacityNotch.app`
in Applications with the new one, and open it. macOS will likely ask for
**Open Anyway** in System Settings → Privacy & Security again, since this
build is not notarized. The permissions you granted are kept.

What's new:

- **New Settings.** A window with a sidebar — General, Providers, Alerts,
  Modules, Diagnostics — and ⌘1–⌘5 to move between them. Each Provider has a
  card with its state, when it was last read, why it is not being read when it
  is not, and a Refresh button. Music now lives under Modules, where more will
  join it. Settings follows the Mac's light or dark appearance, or the one you
  choose in General.
- **A shorter menu.** Refresh Now, Settings… and Quit; turning Providers on
  and off is in Settings.
- **The notch, redrawn.** Every size and colour now matches the design; it
  opens and closes on springs, and the music page follows two fingers when you
  swipe.
- **Out of the way in fullscreen.** Over a fullscreen app the closed notch
  shows only Capacity, without the music row; hovering still opens it.
- **Music.** The equalizer moves more naturally and rests as dashes when the
  track is paused, and the track's time counts every second.

Built with `./Scripts/build-release.sh` from the tagged commit; the same
commit and Swift toolchain produce the same archive.
