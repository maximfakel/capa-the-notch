Capacity Notch 0.2.2, a beta for Apple Silicon on macOS 14 or later.

**Updating from 0.2.1:** quit the running copy, replace `CapacityNotch.app`
in Applications with the new one, and open it. macOS will likely ask for
**Open Anyway** in System Settings → Privacy & Security again, since this
build is not notarized. The permissions you granted are kept.

What's fixed:

- **Dictation works with Bluetooth headphones.** With a headset such as the
  Sony WH-1000XM4, holding the shortcut could freeze Capacity Notch, or
  record silence and keep recording silence after the headphones were
  gone, until it was reopened. The microphone is now read on its own,
  without opening the headphones' output, and a headset that connects or
  leaves during a recording is followed without losing what was said.

What's new:

- **The notch and its notifications in Russian.** With Russian chosen in
  Settings → General, the cards, the Music and Teleprompter rows, the
  Dictation capsule, the capacity alert and what VoiceOver reads are in
  Russian, and change the moment the language does. Onboarding stays in
  English for now.
- **The log hears the microphone.** With "Keep a log for bug reports" on,
  it records the input's rate and channels, a change of input during a
  recording, and how much was recorded before recognition.
