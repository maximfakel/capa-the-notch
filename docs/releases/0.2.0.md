Capacity Notch 0.2.0, a beta for Apple Silicon on macOS 14 or later.

**Updating from 0.1.5:** quit the running copy, replace `CapacityNotch.app`
in Applications with the new one, and open it. macOS will likely ask for
**Open Anyway** in System Settings → Privacy & Security again, since this
build is not notarized. The permissions you granted are kept.

What's new — two Modules, both off until you turn them on in Settings →
Modules:

- **Teleprompter.** Paste a Script and it scrolls in the notch, beside the
  camera, so you read while looking into it. Global shortcuts start, pause
  and change its speed.
- **Dictation, on this Mac.** Hold ⌃⌥D, speak Russian, release: the text is
  typed where your cursor is, or left on the clipboard if there is nowhere
  to type. Esc cancels. Recognition runs locally with the GigaAM model — a
  separate 170 MB download from Settings — and audio is never saved. Common
  IT terms said in Russian ("диплой", "коммит") are replaced with their
  English spelling, and the rules can be edited. History is optional and off
  by default. A glowing orb below the surface shows listening, recognising,
  done and errors. It asks for the microphone; Accessibility is optional and
  makes typing into other applications more reliable.

What's fixed:

- **"Appear in screen sharing and recordings" sticks.** On macOS 27, once
  the surface had been hidden from a recording — the switch turned off and
  on, or a Script read — it stayed hidden until Capacity Notch was reopened.
