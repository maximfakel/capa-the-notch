Capacity Notch 0.2.1, a beta for Apple Silicon on macOS 14 or later.

**Updating from 0.2.0:** quit the running copy, replace `CapacityNotch.app`
in Applications with the new one, and open it. macOS will likely ask for
**Open Anyway** in System Settings → Privacy & Security again, since this
build is not notarized. The permissions you granted are kept.

What's fixed — both found on other people's Macs, both only in the
published build:

- **Dictation asks for the microphone.** 0.2.0 was signed without its
  microphone entitlement, so macOS refused it silently: no prompt, and no
  Capacity Notch in Privacy & Security → Microphone. Press **Allow
  microphone access** again after updating.
- **The speech model shows its download.** Setup stayed at 0% for the whole
  170 MB, as if nothing were happening. The download itself was running.
- **Source & licences** opens the licences again.

What's new:

- **Settings and the menu in Russian.** Choose the language in Settings →
  General: the Mac's own, English or Russian. The notch and notifications
  stay in English for now.
- **Settings are set in Geist.** The menu keeps the system's font.
- **The log hears Dictation.** With "Keep a log for bug reports" on, the log
  records the model download, the microphone request and its answer,
  recording, recognition and insertion — as states and error codes, never
  audio or text. Copy Diagnostics notes Dictation's state, including a build
  that cannot ask for the microphone.
