Capacity Notch 0.2.9, a beta for Apple Silicon on macOS 14 or later.

**Updating from 0.2.8:** quit the running copy, replace `CapacityNotch.app`
in Applications with the new one, and open it. macOS will likely ask for
**Open Anyway** in System Settings → Privacy & Security again, since this
build is not notarized. The permissions you granted are kept.

What's new:

- **The Shelf.** A new Module, off until you turn it on in Settings →
  Modules. Drag a file towards the closed notch: the strip gives a little
  and a drop tab grows beneath it. Let go, and the file waits on the Shelf,
  the open surface's new page, until you drag it somewhere else — where it
  is moved as Finder would move it. Up to twenty files; the Shelf empties
  when Capacity Notch quits and keeps only references, never copies.
- **Images and files from the clipboard.** On a switch of their own, off by
  default: a screenshot taken to the clipboard, an image copied from a
  page, media or a document copied in a messenger lands on the Shelf by
  itself. Images with no file of their own are held in memory and become a
  file where you drop them. Copying in Finder, text and passwords are left
  alone.
- **Page buttons.** With four pages to move between, the dots under the
  open surface grow into buttons with each page's icon as the pointer comes
  near, and a click goes straight to that page.
