Capacity Notch 0.2.3, a beta for Apple Silicon on macOS 14 or later.

**Updating from 0.2.2:** quit the running copy, replace `CapacityNotch.app`
in Applications with the new one, and open it. macOS will likely ask for
**Open Anyway** in System Settings → Privacy & Security again, since this
build is not notarized. The permissions you granted are kept.

What's new — the closed strip:

- **Both Providers on:** each side shows its Provider's five-hour window,
  so the strip reads the same two windows every time. Settings → Providers
  → "Shown in the closed strip" switches it to the week, or to whichever
  window has least left.
- **One Provider on:** it takes both sides — its five hours on the left,
  its week on the right, each with its own pace.
- **No Provider on:** the strip is empty and the surface stays open on the
  Connect cards until one is turned on.
- In Russian, the cards say "Исп" for used, to fit.
